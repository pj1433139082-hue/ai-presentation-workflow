VISUAL CONCEPT

LAYOUT AND LEGIBILITY

Use generous white space, simple wayfinding symbols, and high-contrast readable Chinese typography. Make every required line large enough to read at presentation size. Keep all copy inside the canvas.

ART DIRECTION
A calm civic-culture information design: warm white paper, deep ink-blue typography, clear cobalt and teal route accents, and small touches of marigold and coral. Blend crisp flat illustration with lightly textured printmaking or colored-paper shapes. Friendly and dignified, current and accessible; not childish, not corporate, not an official government template. Use direct, simple visual cues rather than decorative clutter.
