# Generation prompt

Create one complete 16:9 landscape editorial product-story page for an entirely fictional consumer tea brand. This is a packaging concept proposal page, not a generic brand cover and not a real retail product advertisement.

ART DIRECTION
A tactile, sunlit still-life scene on warm uncoated paper: an elegant rectangular tea carton with an abstract contour-line motif, one open carton revealing individually wrapped tea-sample sachets, a small ceramic cup of amber tea, and a human hand gently pouring hot water beside a folded linen cloth. The scene should tell a small sequence at a glance: open the box, take a sachet, brew a quiet cup. Show believable paperboard, softly fibred cotton paper, subtle blind embossing, ceramic glaze, tea color, and morning window light. No real landscape or map, no recognizable regional landmark, no named tea variety or growing location. Keep it intimate and editorial, with a polished lifestyle photograph mixed with subtle paper-collage details and loose, hand-drawn contour accents.

LAYOUT
Landscape 16:9, designed as a finished product-story page that could sit inside a brand proposal. Make an asymmetrical magazine composition with generous open paper at upper left for brand and title, a large tactile product scene across the center and right, and three small precise annotation labels placed beside the corresponding package details. Labels may connect with fine hairline leaders, but do not put them in a row of identical cards. The package, its materials, and the brewing moment must be clearly visible. Let the image itself carry the story, with no dense small print.

COLOR AND TYPE
Use warm parchment, dark tea-brown ink, a restrained cinnabar-red accent, deep plum shadows, and transparent amber tea. Let sunlight and material texture create depth. Use a refined Chinese editorial Song-style display face for the title, balanced with quiet modern Chinese sans-serif for labels. Keep the page warm, literary, and contemporary; avoid a generic Japanese matcha mood, bright green branding, rigid grids, glossy plastic, and excessive decoration.

慢下来。"

Required disclosure, clearly readable:

Keep every Chinese character accurate and clean. Keep the disclosure legible and do not crop it. The abstract mountain contour on the carton is a graphic motif only and must not imply where tea is grown. The final image should read as a specific product story page about packaging, materials, and a calm brewing moment, not just a title page.
